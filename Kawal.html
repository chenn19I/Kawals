Nine rules, one verdict.

  • R1REJECTSimulation failed or timed out
  • R2REJECTRecipient is on the reject list
  • R3HOLDTotal outflow exceeds personal limit
  • R4HOLDNew recipient, amount above the limit
  • R5HOLDCalls an unknown Move package
  • R6HOLDTransfers an object other than a coin
  • R7HOLDRecipient flagged as dangerous by a data source
  • R8PASSTrusted recipient, amount under the limit
  • R9HOLDNo rule matched

Default limits: 5 SUI total outflow, 1 SUI new recipient, 5 SUI trusted recipient.
